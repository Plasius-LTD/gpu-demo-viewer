Camera help

Drag to orbit. Shift-drag or right-drag to truck. Alt-drag to pan. Scroll to move closer; Shift-scroll changes the lens. Touch: one finger orbits; two fingers move and pinch. Keyboard: arrows orbit, Shift-arrows truck, + / − move closer or further, Home resets. Keys 1–5 choose orbit, truck, pan, dolly or zoom. Press ? for a brief reminder.

Review focus

This is an inspection pass, not a visual-quality approval. Source animations are retained on disk; this view displays static geometry.

Render quality

Fixed sampling: every pixel progressively accumulates the selected target.

Hold the camera still to refine. Higher targets reduce noise; more bounces reveal indirect light. HD, QHD and 4K trace at the selected size, even in a smaller window. Higher resolution costs more GPU time; use one sample for a quick inspection.
